# LinkedIn Profile for a Fresher BA

## Definition

Your **LinkedIn profile** is the public version of the resume: **headline**, **About**, **skills**, **projects**, plus Featured links. For a fresher it must say BA *intent* and *evidence* without fake job titles at ShopEase.

## Why it matters

Recruiters source on LinkedIn. A headline “Student at XYZ” hides you. A headline with Business Analyst + SQL + a domain makes you searchable. About and Projects do the 30-second sell.

## LinkedIn headline

Pattern: `{Role intent} | {2–3 proof skills} | {domain or tools}`

**Sample headlines**

- Aspiring Business Analyst | Requirements, Process Mapping, SQL | E-commerce & Banking case work
- Business Analyst (Fresher) | User Stories, UAT, Power BI | Open to Associate BA roles
- Junior BA candidate | Stakeholder analysis, Excel, Jira | Pune

Avoid: “Open to work | Please connect | Influencer.” Avoid fake “BA at ShopEase.”

## LinkedIn About section

First two lines must work on mobile (truncated). Then proof. Then ask.

**Sample About (fresher BA):**

```text
I am training as a Business Analyst: turning messy operations into scoped
requirements, process maps, and testable stories.

Portfolio (practice cases, not employment):
• ShopEase returns — cut the problem to seller approval delay; auto-approve
  rules, SMS status, SQL/Power BI insight (simulated cycle time 9 → 4.2 days).
• NovaBank salaried loans — incomplete files as the TAT driver; completeness
  checklist, out-of-scope gold loans, hybrid documentation.

Comfortable with elicitation notes, As-Is/To-Be, FR/NFR excerpts, Excel, SQL,
and Power BI. Seeking Associate / Junior BA roles.

GitHub: [link]  |  Happy to walk through a case in 15 minutes.
```

## LinkedIn skills

Add skills you can survive in an interview: Business Analysis, Requirements Gathering, User Stories, SQL, Microsoft Excel, Power BI, Jira, Stakeholder Management, Process Mapping, UAT.

Pin the top 3 to match your target JDs.

### Endorsements strategy (not spam)

| Do | Don’t |
|---|---|
| Ask 3 people who saw your case (classmate, mentor) after they watched a walkthrough | Mass-message “endorse me I’ll endorse you” |
| Endorse others only for skills you have seen | Buy endorsements |
| Treat endorsements as weak signal; Featured project is stronger | 99 skills, 0 projects |

## LinkedIn projects

Create **Projects** (or Featured): ShopEase Returns (Practice Case), NovaBank Loan Origination (Practice Case). Description = 5 lines from the resume. Link GitHub. Dates = when you built it. **No company as employer.**

Featured: PDF one-pager or GitHub `00-story.md`.

### Weak vs strong

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| Headline: “Looking for job” | Headline with BA + SQL + domain |
| About: soft skills only | About: two cases + tools + ask |
| Skills: 50 random | 10 honest, 3 pinned |
| Experience: ShopEase BA intern (untrue) | Project: ShopEase practice case |

## Real-world examples

1. Recruiter search `business analyst SQL Pune` hits your headline; they open Featured ShopEase story.
2. Profile says “Business Analyst at NovaBank” — background check fails later even if you meant “case.”
3. Skills show Salesforce with 20 endorsements from strangers; interviewer asks; you freeze.

## Scenario / Use case: building the profile in one evening

**Context.** Resume is done. LinkedIn still says “Student | Marketing intern hopeful.”

**What you do.**

1. Photo professional, banner simple.
2. Headline + About as above.
3. Add two Projects + Featured GitHub.
4. Skills: 10 real; ask one mentor to endorse *after* they reviewed the case.
5. Experience: only real internships/jobs; campus clubs as Volunteer if true.
6. Open-to-work: recruiters only if you do not want your current internship notified (settings).

**What goes wrong if ignored.** Resume says BA; LinkedIn says student marketer. Sourcers trust LinkedIn.

## Notes

- Headline = BA intent + skills + domain; never fake employer names.
- About = two practice cases, tools, clear ask.
- Projects + Featured beat a wall of unearned endorsements.
- Endorse and request endorsements only where work was seen.
