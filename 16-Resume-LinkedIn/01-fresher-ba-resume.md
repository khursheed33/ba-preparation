# Fresher BA Resume

## Definition

A **fresher BA resume** is a **one-page** document that sells BA skill through **education**, **projects** (not fake jobs), **achievements**, **skills**, and **certifications**.

You do **not** invent employment at ShopEase or NovaBank. Those names are **practice case studies**.

## Why it matters

Screeners give ~10 seconds. A two-page essay of coursework loses to one page of impact bullets tied to artifacts and tools.

## One-page structure (top to bottom)

1. **Name + city + phone + email + LinkedIn + GitHub/portfolio**
2. **Headline:** “Aspiring Business Analyst | Requirements, process mapping, SQL”
3. **Summary (3 lines):** what you can do, domains practiced, not “hardworking team player”
4. **Skills:** grouped (BA | data | tools) — words that match JDs
5. **Projects** (largest section)
6. **Education**
7. **Certifications** (real only)
8. **Achievements** (hackathon, college BA club, measurable)

No photo, no tables that break ATS (see next topic), no “Objective: seeking a challenging position.”

## Education section

- Degree, institute, year, relevant coursework (1 line): DBMS, stats, if true.
- Do not list class 10 marks unless the JD asks.

## Project section (practice cases as PROJECTS)

**ShopEase — Returns cycle-time (practice case)**  
- Mapped As-Is/To-Be for prepaid returns; identified seller approval (2.1 days) as the main wait.  
- Wrote FR/BR for auto-approve of Size returns < ₹2,000 plus status SMS; kept chatbot out of scope.  
- Built Excel/SQL sample and a Power BI view of delay-by-reason; recommended sequencing by volume not max average.  
- Produced stories, AC, RTM excerpt, and UAT scenarios (simulated impact: 9 → 4.2 days).

**NovaBank — Personal loan origination (practice case)**  
- Defined problem: 10-day TAT with 34% files stalling on documents (sandbox-style counts).  
- Elicited completeness rules; gold loans out of scope; hybrid baseline + sprint slices.  
- Specified checklist + status SMS; documented bureau API as dependency/NFR, not “AI underwriting.”

Label **Practice case** or **Academic/portfolio project** so ATS and humans do not read it as employment.

## Achievement section

Weak: “Participated in many events.”  
Strong: “Led a 4-person campus process-mapping mini-project; cut mock approval steps from 9 to 6 in the To-Be (presentation, 2026).”

## Skills section

BA: elicitation, process mapping, user stories, AC, UAT, stakeholder mapping  
Data: Excel (COUNTIFS, Pivot), SQL (JOIN, GROUP BY), Power BI  
Tools: Jira, Confluence, draw.io/BPMN, GitHub  
Do not list “C++ expert” unless you will survive a question.

## Certifications

Only finished ones (e.g. Google Data Analytics, ECBA if you have it). “In progress” = one line max.

## Weak vs strong bullets

| Weak | Strong |
|---|---|
| Responsible for documentation | Wrote 12 FRs and a rule table for ShopEase auto-approve (practice case) |
| Used SQL | Wrote a DISTINCT count of stalled NovaBank apps vs a known monthly volume |
| Team player | Facilitated a mock workshop; captured decision log and open questions |
| Knowledge of Agile | Split a baseline KYC rule from sprint-sized SMS stories |

## Real-world examples

1. Candidate lists “Business Analyst, ShopEase, 2025–2026” — recruiter verifies, reject for integrity.
2. Same content under **Projects** with GitHub link — interview scheduled.
3. Two-page resume of every lab — ATS + human both bounce.

## Scenario / Use case: converting a case study into resume lines

**Context.** You have a 15-page ShopEase pack. Resume has 8 lines total for projects.

**What you do.** Pick bottleneck, scope judgment, artifacts, tool, metric (simulated). Drop the glossary. Keep “practice case” in the title.

**What goes wrong if ignored.** Resume looks like a job history you do not have, or like a student who never shipped an artifact.

## Notes

- One page; projects not fake employment; ShopEase/NovaBank are case titles.
- Bullets = impact + artifact + tool.
- Education short; skills grouped; certifications only if real.
- Integrity beats a famous company name in the experience section.
